## Approving this agreement electronically

You have been asked to approve this agreement, not to sign it as a party. Please read this
short notice first.

### What your approval is

When you tick the boxes and choose **Approve this agreement**, you are recording your
electronic approval of this document as it stands. You are not signing it as one of its
parties. If the agreement asks for an approval mark, the mark you draw or type is displayed on
it, but it is not what makes the approval — your deliberate act of approving is. We record what
you were shown, what you filled in, when you approved, and how you reached this page.

We do not issue you a personal certificate, and we do not check who you are. Reaching this
page means somebody with access to the invited mailbox opened the link we sent, and, if this
agreement was set up to ask for one, entered a code we sent to that same address. That shows
control of a mailbox. It is not proof of identity, and nothing here claims to be an identity
check.

### What happens to the document

Once everybody has signed or approved, the finished PDF is sealed with a certificate belonging
to this service. The seal is what lets a reader detect whether the finished document has been
altered since. It belongs to the service, not to you — it is not a personal certificate, and it
does not represent an eIDAS advanced or qualified electronic signature. The completion report
bound into the document lists your approval separately from the parties who signed.

### What we keep

Alongside your approval we keep a record of this session: the version of this notice you were
shown, the exact document and field values you were shown, the time the server accepted your
approval, the network address and browser description your request arrived with, and whether a
mailed code was used. The network address and browser description corroborate the session;
they are not treated as proof of who you are.

### Your choices

- You can decline. There is an option on this page to say you do not want to approve, and using
  it stops the agreement for everybody. Closing the page or ignoring the invitation approves
  nothing.
- You can ask the sender for a copy before you approve. Everyone on the agreement gets a copy of
  the sealed PDF once it is complete.
- If you cannot use a mouse or a touchscreen, type your name instead of drawing it. The typed
  option carries exactly the same weight as the drawn one.

### If something looks wrong

If the document is not what you expected, or a field has been filled in with something you did
not supply, do not approve it. Contact the sender. A correction is a new version of the
agreement and needs fresh signatures and approvals from everyone.
